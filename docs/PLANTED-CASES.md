# Planted cases in the sample data

The sample data (`data/sample-plan-data.js`, generated by `tools/generate-sample-data.js`) contains deliberate cases so that:
- every insight rule fires at least once (US-1.3.2, US-1.7.1);
- every missing-data state is visible (US-1.2.11);
- the demo has something worth showing on every chart.

This file is the **contract between the DATA stream** (which plants the cases) **and the INSIGHTS stream** (which finds them). Tests check each planted case produces its expected insight (TPV-TC-198). If a case has to change, change it here first, then in the generator and the tests.

All names and figures are fictional. Money is in thousands of EUR.

- **Tier 1 is fixed by group strategy**: the three group-priority industries are Tier 1 in every region, and no other industry is Tier 1. Leaders choose between Tier 2 and Tier 3 for the rest.

**Scores:**
- Attractiveness = mean of growth potential, criticality and competitive intensity.
- Ability to win = mean of references, expertise and product fit.
- Both run from 1 to 3; the midpoint is 2.0.
- "Low ability" means an ability score below 2.0; "attractive" means an attractiveness score of 2.0 or more.

## Consistency the generator guarantees (TPV-TC-195)

- New Business: `arrPotential[0]` = targetAccounts × hitRate × avgDealSize. Year 2 = year 1 × (1 + growth.year2), and year 3 = year 2 × (1 + growth.year3). Services potential = ARR potential × servicesRatio. Channel splits sum to 1.
- New Business `tier` matches the region's Market Coverage tier for that industry, and is never 3.
- Customer Growth: incrementalArr comes from growthPct year by year (or from the 3-year multiplier, spread evenly). servicesOrderIntake = incrementalArr × servicesRatio. cumulativeOrderIntake = the sum of both. The segment follows the thresholds rule in the Planning Template Structure.
- Every list item has `sourceRow`; recap items have `sourceCell`; `meta.sourceMap` covers every field the views read. Its shape follows `tests/fixtures/mini-data.js`.
- The same settings always give the same file (seeded random numbers).

## Expectations block

The generator writes a comment block at the top of the data file listing the computed figures each planted case relies on:
- P06 pipeline share, P08 rates, P09 deal sizes, P10 ratio, P12 wins, P13–P15 shares;
- the per-region attractive-but-not-yet lists;
- the headline sentence's figures (TPV-TC-099).

Tests read the figures from `window.SAMPLE_EXPECT`, which the generator writes in `tests/fixtures/sample-expected.js`. The tests never recompute them.
