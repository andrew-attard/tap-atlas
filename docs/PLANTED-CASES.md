# Planted cases in the sample data

The sample data (`data/sample-plan-data.js`, generated by `tools/generate-sample-data.js`) contains deliberate cases so that:
- every insight rule fires at least once (US-1.3.2, US-1.7.1);
- every missing-data state is visible (US-1.2.11);
- the demo has something worth showing on every chart.

This file is the **contract between the DATA stream** (which plants the cases) **and the INSIGHTS stream** (which finds them). Tests check each planted case produces its expected insight (TPV-TC-198). If a case has to change, change it here first, then in the generator and the tests.

All names and figures are fictional. Money is in thousands of EUR.

## Phase 4 planted cases: the full template (US-4.1.3)

Planted by `tools/sample-p4.js` from the settings in `tools/sample-planted-p4.js`, and checked by `tools/sample-expect-p4.js` (the generator refuses to write a file where a case or an add-up rule doesn't hold). The Phase 4 parts are added last and draw no random numbers, so **every figure of the earlier phases is unchanged**. The one existing field that changes is each partner's `maturity`, which now holds a level from `lookups.partnerMaturity` instead of free text.

How the new parts are built, so the figures add up:
- **Solutions.** Six solutions, "Solution 1" to "Solution 6": 1 to 4 are recurring, Solution 5 is perpetual software and Solution 6 is hardware. Each New Business row names one, handed out so each region lands near its target mix.
- **Books value, ARR and services** (`arr`, `services`) is the recap's customer value, less the reseller's margin on what the region's partners distribute and less the services those partners deliver themselves (the region's outsourcing %). Through the own sales force it equals customer value; it is never above it.
- **Books value, perpetual software and hardware** (`swPerpetual`, `hardware`) is the one-off licence or hardware sold with each new deal of Solution 5 or Solution 6: 80% and 60% of that solution's new recurring order intake through the books. It runs through the books only. The recap holds ARR and services, so **the gap between customer value and books value is taken over ARR and services** (ARCHITECTURE section 19.2), and the books total of a region can be above its customer value.
- **Routes** split the same books order intake another way, so each year's routes add up to its books value: own sales force (direct new business), customer success and partner existing business (existing customers), Alliance B as reseller, and the Partner and Alliance A channels split between other resellers and system integrators by their partners' types.
- **Revenue** is a share of the same year's order intake (40%, 48% and 55% of ARR and 70%, 78% and 85% of services for years 1 to 3, times a factor per region), so it is never above it.
- **Strategic plan and base year** are set against the plan's books order intake: the strategic plan from each region's planted distance to it, the base-year forecast from the planted growth into year 1. Each `variance` is the books order intake for that year and type minus the strategic plan.

**"The plan"** below is the plan's order intake through the books (`booksValue`, all four product categories), over the three plan years unless a year is named. Shares and ratios are a ratio of sums. ## Consistency the generator guarantees (TPV-TC-195)

- New Business: `arrPotential[0]` = targetAccounts × hitRate × avgDealSize. Year 2 = year 1 × (1 + growth.year2), and year 3 = year 2 × (1 + growth.year3). Services potential = ARR potential × servicesRatio. Channel splits sum to 1.
- New Business `tier` matches the region's Market Coverage tier for that industry, and is never 3.
- Customer Growth: incrementalArr comes from growthPct year by year (or from the 3-year multiplier, spread evenly). servicesOrderIntake = incrementalArr × servicesRatio. cumulativeOrderIntake = the sum of both. The segment follows the thresholds rule in the Planning Template Structure.
- Every list item has `sourceRow`; recap items have `sourceCell`; `meta.sourceMap` covers every field the views read. Its shape follows `tests/fixtures/mini-data.js`.
- The same settings always give the same file (seeded random numbers).
- Phase 4 (TPV-TC-659): for every region and year, the four product categories of `booksValue` add up to the books total, and the `routes` add up to the same total. Over ARR and services, books value equals customer value through the own sales force and is never above it for any channel. Revenue is never above the order intake of the same year, channel and motion. Every strategic plan `variance` is the books order intake for that year and type minus the strategic plan, and every `coverage` the workbook gives is pipeline over forecast minus actuals. The sample loads with one warning only: Central Europe's empty Customer Growth section (G2).

## Expectations block

The generator writes a comment block at the top of the data file listing the computed figures each planted case relies on:
- P06 pipeline share, P08 rates, P09 deal sizes, P10 ratio, P12 wins, P13–P15 shares;
- the per-region attractive-but-not-yet lists;
- the headline sentence's figures (TPV-TC-099);
- the Phase 4 cases R01 to R10, one line each.

Tests read the figures from `window.SAMPLE_EXPECT`, which the generator writes in `tests/fixtures/sample-expected.js`. The tests never recompute them.
